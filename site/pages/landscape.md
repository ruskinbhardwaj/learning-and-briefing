## Questions this site tracks

- **Autonomous SDLC:** Which stages of software delivery can agents own end to end today, and what still needs a person?
- **AI-native operating models:** How are companies breaking down "become AI-native" into concrete changes, and which ones are working?
- **Products & companies:** What is shipping, who is winning which layer, and where do the flywheels sit?
- **Geopolitics:** How do export controls, sovereignty pushes and regulation reshape who can build what?
- **Enabling tech:** Where are the bottlenecks (chips, power, sites, capital) and what could loosen them?
- **Humans & AI:** How is AI changing how mathematics and science are done, and what role are people likely to play alongside it?
