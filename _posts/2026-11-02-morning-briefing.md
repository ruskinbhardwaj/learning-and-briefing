*Covers 30 October to 2 November, including the weekend.*

**Today in one line:** Intel's collapse turned a week of AI spending worries into a sharp tech sell-off to close October.

### Intel fell about 30% after a weak outlook, its worst day since the 1970s
<span class="meta">Enabling tech</span>

Intel shares fell roughly 30% on 30 October after its third-quarter results and a weak outlook for the current quarter ([Quartz](https://qz.com/intel-stock-plunges-amazon-nasdaq-100-sell-off-103026), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/fionariley/2026/10/30/intel-sinks-29-on-earnings-as-ai-spending-fears-worsen-tech-selloff/)). CNBC called it the stock's worst day since the 1970s, and the drop wiped about $12 billion off the value of the US government's stake in the company ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/30/amazon-intel-earnings-big-tech-ai-spending-concerns.html), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/30/intels-stock-crashes-knocking-12b-from-governments-stake/)).

**Why it matters:** Intel is America's main bet on making leading-edge chips at home rather than relying on Taiwan, which is why Washington took a stake. A collapse in its value raises the political cost of that bet and questions whether the US can close the manufacturing gap. It also shows how unevenly the AI boom pays: companies selling AI chips have thrived, while Intel has struggled to win that business.

### Tech closed October with a sharp sell-off, and Amazon fell after missing expectations
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

The Nasdaq and S&P 500 fell sharply on 30 October as Intel and Amazon led tech lower ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-nasdaq-p-123540419.html), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-10-30/tech-selloff-deepens-as-intel-and-amazon-shares-fall-video)). Amazon missed expectations for revenue and earnings, even though AWS grew 39% ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/10/30/stock-market-today-oct-30-amazon-misses-revenue-and-earnings-expectations/)).
