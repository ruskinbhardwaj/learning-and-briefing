*Covers 3 to 4 November.*

**Today in one line:** the AI build-out now has to win votes as well as funding, nationally and in local town halls.

### Democrats won control of the House and Senate
<span class="meta">Geopolitics</span>

Democrats won control of both the House and the Senate in the 3 November midterm elections, taking them from Republicans ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/election-day-2026-midterms/), [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/live/midterms-2026-live-updates-election-day-voters-head-to-the-polls-as-democrats-look-to-flip-gop-control-of-the-house-and-senate-110000715.html)). The new Congress takes office in January.

**Why it matters:** Congress writes the laws and funding behind chip export controls, energy permitting, subsidies for domestic chipmaking and any federal AI rules. A change of control means a new set of committee chairs setting that agenda. What they will actually prioritise isn't yet known, and we won't speculate before they say.

### Voters in several places rejected data centers
<span class="meta">Enabling tech</span>

Data center measures were on the ballot in ten states, according to Ballotpedia ([Ballotpedia](https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/11/03/voters-in-ten-states-will-vote-on-data-center-related-ballot-measures-november-3/)). Stateline reported that voters across the country rejected data centers, and communities around Columbus, Ohio, voted against them across several ballot issues ([Stateline](https://stateline.org/2026/11/04/voters-across-the-country-reject-data-centers/), [NBC4 Columbus](https://www.nbc4i.com/news/your-local-election-hq/columbus-area-communities-vote-on-data-center-issues-live-results/)).

**Why it matters:** yesterday's briefing flagged local politics as a new limit on the build-out, and the first results point that way. We haven't seen a full tally of every measure, so we can't yet say how broad the pattern is.

### AMD's data center revenue nearly doubled, and its shares jumped
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

AMD beat expectations for the third quarter, with data center revenue nearly doubling from a year earlier; its shares rose about 8% after hours ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/03/amd-amd-q3-earnings-report-2026-.html), [AMD](https://newsroom.amd.com/news/amd-reports-third-quarter-2026-earnings/)). SiliconANGLE linked the growth to demand from agentic AI workloads ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/03/amd-beats-expectations-as-agentic-ai-sparks-its-data-center-revenue-growth-engines/)).

```
  Last week vs this week, chipmakers
  Intel  ▼ ~30%   weak outlook, struggling to win AI business
  AMD    ▲ ~8%    data center revenue ~2x
```

**Why it matters:** this is the split in the AI boom. Companies selling the chips the build-out runs on are growing fast, while those without a strong AI product are being left behind, even as investors worry about the spending overall.
