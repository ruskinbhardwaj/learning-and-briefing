*Covers 26 to 27 October.*

**Today in one line:** Anthropic opened its books to go public, and they show the economics of a frontier lab: fast-growing revenue and far larger losses.

### Anthropic filed to list on Nasdaq, with $16.3 billion of revenue and an $80.7 billion loss in the first half
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

On 26 October Anthropic, the maker of Claude, publicly filed its S-1, the document a company must publish before selling shares, and plans to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/live/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-frontier-ai-lab-discloses-revenue-and-risks-in-highly-anticipated-s-1-filing-141702607.html), [CNN](https://cnn.com/2026/10/26/tech/anthropic-files-ipo)). The filing shows $16.3 billion of revenue in the first half of 2026 and a net loss of about $80 billion, a figure the Wall Street Journal had first reported from a draft on 21 October ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-ai-s-1-filing.html), [The Star via WSJ](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/10/21/anthropic-logged-net-loss-of-over-80-billion-in-first-half-of-2026-wsj-reports)). CNBC also highlighted a 900% revenue retention figure: existing customers, as a group, are spending many times what they did a year earlier.

```
  Revenue, H1 2026   █▌                    $16.3B
  Net loss, H1 2026  ████████████████      $80.7B
```

**Why it matters:** this is the first time a frontier lab's full accounts are public. The retention figure supports the bull case that customers who adopt AI keep spending more, the opposite of last week's "zero payoff" argument. The loss shows how much compute it takes to stay at the frontier. How public investors price it will shape funding for every other lab and neocloud; CNBC called the possible effect a "trickle-down" across tech ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-ipo-might-trigger-tech-reset-if-ipo-underperforms.html)). We haven't read the filing itself, so we haven't checked how much of the loss is cash.

*Disclosure: this briefing is written by Claude, which Anthropic makes. The facts above come from independent coverage, held to the same standard as any other company.*

### AI stocks rebounded after the filing
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

The Nasdaq and S&P 500 rose on 26 October, with coverage linking the move to the Anthropic filing and renewed optimism about AI after last week's sell-off ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-oct-26-2026), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/10/26/stock-market-today-oct-26-ai-optimism-lifts-nasdaq-as-tech-stocks-rebound/)).

**Why it matters:** one day doesn't reverse a trend. The real test is this week's Big Tech results and the Fed decision.
