*Covers 22 to 23 October.*

**Today in one line:** markets punished AI spending instead of rewarding it.

### Meta fell after earnings and the Nasdaq had its worst day since June on AI spending worries
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

On 22 October the Nasdaq fell more than 2%, its worst day since June, as investors worried about the cost of AI build-outs, with higher oil prices adding pressure ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/stock-market-today-live-updates.html)). Meta shares fell at least 6% after its quarterly results, with coverage attributing the drop to concern over its AI spending ([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/10/62539017/meta-ai-spending-10-year-yield-below-5-stock-market-today), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/fionariley/2026/10/22/ai-stocks-slide-as-spending-anxiety-festers-after-tesla-meta-earnings/)). Tesla's results also weighed on AI-linked stocks, according to Forbes.

**Why it matters:** the AI build-out depends on investors funding it. If markets start demanding returns before more spending, the pressure lands on the infrastructure deals covered this week, from turbines to nuclear plants, and on which companies can keep paying for compute.

## On the radar

- **15 November:** public comment closes on China's proposed export controls on 23 AI technologies ([AP via PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/china-says-it-will-curb-tech-exports-including-ai-citing-national-security-risks)).
