*Covers 6 to 9 November, including the weekend.*

**Today in one line:** the AI sell-off paused on Friday, but its cost is now showing up in the biggest deal on the calendar, Anthropic's IPO.

### AI stocks steadied, but the week was the worst since April 2025
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

On Friday 6 November the Nasdaq rose 1.1% and the S&P 500 0.3%, while the Dow fell 0.5% ([Spectrum News via AP](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/nys/binghamton/business/2026/11/06/the-worldwide-sell-off-for-ai-stocks-halts)). Nvidia gained 2.8% after falling 9.3% and 3.2% on the two days before, and CoreWeave rose 6.3%. The S&P 500 still lost 2.7% for the week, its worst since the April 2025 tariff shock, and ended 5.7% below last month's record. Tokyo and Seoul had fallen 6% earlier in the session. The same morning, the government reported that employers added 165,000 jobs in October, with unemployment at 4.1% ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm)). AP said strong hiring made traders see a better chance of a Fed rate *hike* in December, and the 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.86%.

```
  The AI trade, last week
  Wed 4 Nov  ▼     data center stocks fall after midterms
  Thu 5 Nov  ▼▼▼   S&P −4.4%, Nasdaq ≈ −5%
  Fri 6 Nov  ▲     Nasdaq +1.1%, Nvidia +2.8%
  Week          S&P −2.7%: worst since April 2025
```

**Why it matters:** one up day is a pause, not a recovery, and the jobs data cut the other way. Before the report, markets had been hoping for rate cuts to soften an AI slowdown. A strong economy that raises the chance of a hike makes expensive, long-payoff bets like data centers harder to finance.

### Buyers are pushing back on Anthropic's IPO
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

*Disclosure: this briefing is written by Claude, which Anthropic makes.*

Two reports on 6 November said the sell-off has reached Anthropic's planned listing. Reuters, citing four people familiar with investor talks, said potential buyers doubt Anthropic can hold its roadshow, the series of pitches to investors before pricing, as planned and still list in November ([BNN Bloomberg via Reuters](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/artificial-intelligence/2026/11/06/ai-backlash-could-threaten-anthropic-ipo-timing-potential-investors-say-reuters-exclusive/)). Reuters said the roadshow had been expected to start the week of 9 November, while other coverage gives the week of 16 November ([24/7 Wall St.](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/anthropic-could-postpone-1-5-155825407.html)), so the schedule itself is unclear. Separately, The Wall Street Journal reported that three large investors expect the valuation to come down from the $1.5 trillion discussed, one suggesting closer to $1 trillion and another raising a delay ([Decrypt, citing the WSJ](https://decrypt.co/382716/anthropic-ipo-valuation-could-fall-to-1-trillion-after-ai-stock-rout-report)). Reuters said investors also want the October revenue figure, which is blank in the updated filing. Anthropic declined to comment to Reuters, and no price range has been set.

```
  Talked-about valuation for Anthropic's IPO
  Sep  draft filing (reported)   ≈ $2.0T  ██████████
  Oct  discussed                 ≈ $1.5T  ███████▌
  Nov  some buyers' view         ≈ $1.0T  █████
  May  last private round          $965B  ████▊
```

**Why it matters:** at $1 trillion, the IPO would price close to the $965 billion valuation of Anthropic's last private round in May ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-raises-65-billion-nears-1t-valuation-ahead-of-ipo/), background), which would wipe out most of the gain investors had expected from going public. These are buyers' views reported before any price is set, and buyers gain from a lower price, so read them as a negotiating position as well as a forecast. Whatever Anthropic decides, it will show how much money public markets are willing to put into frontier AI labs right now.
