*Covers 29 to 30 October.*

**Today in one line:** the cloud giants' AI businesses are booming, and so is the cash it costs to build them.

### AWS grew 39%, but Amazon's free cash flow was negative $42.4 billion
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

Amazon Web Services revenue grew 39% from a year earlier, beating forecasts ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/29/aws-q3-earnings-report-2026.html), [Quartz](https://qz.com/amazon-aws-cloud-growth-fastest-earnings-102926)). Amazon reported negative free cash flow of $42.4 billion and kept its 2026 capital spending plan unchanged ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/29/amazon-amzn-q3-earnings-report-2026.html)). Its shares fell after the report, with coverage citing spending concerns ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/10/29/stock-market-today-oct-29-amazon-falls-on-q3-earnings-report-and-capex-concerns/)).

```
  This week's cloud results
              Cloud growth      Free cash flow       Shares after results
  Microsoft   strong            —                    up
  Alphabet    ~2x (Google Cloud) negative            down sharply
  Amazon      +39% (AWS)        −$42.4B              down
```

**Why it matters:** this is the pattern of the week. Demand for AI cloud is real at all three companies, but building the data centers costs more cash than the business brings in today. Investors are now judging each company on how long that gap lasts, not on whether demand exists.

### The Nasdaq fell more than 1% as Alphabet's slide hit the AI trade
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

The Nasdaq closed more than 1% lower on 29 October, led down by Alphabet after its results ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/us-stock-futures-drop-as-alphabet-slides-on-soft-earnings-microsoft-firms-4981271), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-nasdaq-futures-104943461.html)).

**Why it matters:** Monday's rebound after Anthropic's IPO filing has faded. The market is swinging day to day on each new piece of evidence about whether AI spending pays back.
