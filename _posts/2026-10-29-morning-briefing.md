*Covers 28 to 29 October.*

**Today in one line:** markets now reward AI spending that already shows up in revenue, and punish spending that eats cash with more to come.

### Microsoft rose and Alphabet fell after both reported strong cloud growth
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

Microsoft beat expectations on revenue and earnings, driven by cloud growth, and its shares rose about 4% ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/microsoft-msft-q1-earnings-report-2027.html), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/live/microsoft-earnings-live-microsoft-beats-q1-expectations-on-top-and-bottom-line-on-ai-growth-204510452.html)). Alphabet's Google Cloud revenue roughly doubled from a year earlier to about $31 billion, but free cash flow was negative ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/28/alphabet-google-stock-drops-cloud-revenue-doubles-free-cash-flow-negative/)). Alphabet kept its 2026 capital spending plan unchanged, said it expects a significant increase in 2027, and its shares fell about 10% ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/alphabet-googl-earnings-q3-2026.html)).

```
              Cloud      Free cash flow   Spending ahead       Shares
  Microsoft   strong     —                —                    ▲ ~4%
  Alphabet    ~2x        negative         big rise in 2027     ▼ ~10%
```

**Why it matters:** both companies are winning AI demand, so the market's verdict turned on cash. Alphabet is selling more cloud than ever but spending faster than it earns, and it signalled more spending ahead. This is last week's "show me the payoff" mood applied to the biggest builders. Amazon reports today.

### The Fed held rates steady in a split vote
<span class="meta">Enabling tech</span>

The Federal Reserve kept its target range at 3.75% to 4.00% on 28 October, in a split vote ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20261028a.htm), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/28/fed-interest-rate-decision-october-2026.html), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/live/fed-meeting-live-updates-interest-rates-held-steady-in-split-vote-warsh-says-politics-played-no-role-ahead-of-midterm-elections-191946928.html)).

**Why it matters:** much of the data center and neocloud build-out is financed with debt. Rates that stay put leave borrowing costs high for the companies that can least afford it, the same pressure covered on Monday.
