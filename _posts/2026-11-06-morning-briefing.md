*Covers 5 to 6 November.*

**Today in one line:** the AI trade had its worst day in this run of briefings, as investors priced in political limits on the build-out.

### AI stocks crashed on fears of a crackdown after the midterms
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

The S&P 500 fell 4.4% on 5 November in a broad sell-off of AI stocks ([NBC Bay Area via AP](https://www.nbcbayarea.com/news/national-international/november-5-stock-market/4170982/)). The Nasdaq dropped about 5% and the Dow lost more than 1,800 points ([Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/markets/stocks-nov-5-2026)). NBC News and NPR tied the fall to the election outcome and worries about AI, and NPR put the loss in tech stocks at about $2 trillion, driven by fears of an "AI crackdown" ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/tech-stocks-plunge-ai-midterms-rcna607650), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/05/nx-s1-6031987/stock-markets-ai-midterm-elections)).

```
  Two weeks of the AI trade
  22 Oct  ▼   Meta falls on spending; Nasdaq's worst day since June
  26 Oct  ▲   Anthropic files to go public
  29–30 Oct ▼ Alphabet, Amazon, Intel results; sharp sell-off
  4 Nov   ▼   data center stocks fall after midterms
  5 Nov   ▼▼▼ S&P −4.4%, Nasdaq ≈ −5%
```

**Why it matters:** two worries have now merged. One is whether AI spending pays off, building since Meta's results on 22 October. The other is whether the new Congress and state governments will slow data centers, which began on 4 November. Markets are pricing both at once. "Crackdown" describes what investors fear, not anything lawmakers have proposed; the new Congress doesn't sit until January. For companies that need to raise money, including those planning to go public, the window just got harder.
