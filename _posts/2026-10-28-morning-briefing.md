*Covers 27 to 28 October.*

**Today in one line:** as AI agents move into real operations, the hard problems are becoming control and risk, not capability.

### Anthropic's IPO filing disclosed a month-long training pause and security breaches
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

Reading through the risk section of Anthropic's S-1, Reuters reported that the company disclosed a training pause lasting about a month and security breaches involving its AI systems ([The Star via Reuters](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/10/27/anthropic-discloses-month-long-training-pause-ai-security-breaches-in-ipo-filing), [Rappler](https://www.rappler.com/technology/anthropic-discloses-training-pause-ai-security-breaches-ipo-filing/)). Fortune noted that the risk factors also cover existential risk from AI, the company's dealings with the Pentagon, and dependence on chief executive Dario Amodei's reputation ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-s1-risk-factors-existential-risk-pentagon-dario-amodei/)).

**Why it matters:** an S-1 forces a company to list in writing what could go wrong, under legal liability. It gives outsiders their first formal view of operational risks that frontier labs usually keep private, such as halting training and having systems breached. Expect rivals to face the same questions when they list. We haven't read the filing ourselves, so we report only what the coverage attributes to it.

### IBM and SAP pitched ways to govern AI agents inside companies
<span class="meta">AI-native operating models</span>

On 27 October IBM announced identity and access controls for AI agents, so each agent can be identified, given permissions and audited like an employee or service account ([IBM](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-10-27-ibm-introduces-identity-and-control-for-ai-agents,-securing-frontier-intelligence-for-the-enterprise)). The same day SAP extended its Joule agents with more governance as part of what it calls the autonomous enterprise ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/27/sap-advances-its-autonomous-enterprise-push-with-smarter-governed-joule-agents)).

```
  Agent ──who are you?──▶ Identity
        ──what may you do?──▶ Permissions
        ──what did you do?──▶ Audit log
```

**Why it matters:** this is one way companies are breaking down the task of becoming AI-native. Agents get treated as a new kind of worker that needs an identity, limits and a record of its actions before it can touch real systems. The vendors that own these control layers stand to gain, because whoever controls permissions controls which agents a company can use.
