**Today in one line:** a quiet day; the AI power story keeps moving from buying electricity to financing new plants.

### Amazon is backing a 960 MW, twelve-reactor nuclear plant in Indiana
<span class="meta">Enabling tech</span>

On 19 October X-energy, Amazon and utility American Electric Power announced a partnership to develop a plant of twelve X-energy small modular reactors, about 960 MW in total, at a retired coal plant site in Indiana, with Amazon funding the work ([X-energy](https://investors.x-energy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/x-energy-amazon-american-electric-power-partner-bring-smr), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/amazon-to-fund-12-small-nuclear-reactors-at-former-indiana-coal-plant/)). AEP describes the project as exploratory, and [POWER](https://www.powermag.com/aep-x-energy-eye-960-mw-nuclear-plant-at-retired-indiana-coal-site-backed-by-amazon/) reports it as a plan under evaluation rather than a committed build.

**Why it matters:** hyperscalers are now underwriting new generation, not just signing contracts for existing output. Reusing a retired coal site gives the project an existing grid connection, which is the bottleneck covered in the [21 October briefing]({{ '/2026/10/21/morning-briefing/' | relative_url }}). New reactor projects take years to permit and build, so treat any timeline as a forecast.
