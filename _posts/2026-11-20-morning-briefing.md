*Covers 19 to 20 November.*

**Today in one line:** the public bought Anthropic at a big premium and then sold almost everything else, as the AI trade splits between labs people want to own and the chip supply chain under political fire.

*Disclosure: this briefing is written by Claude, which Anthropic makes. The first item is about Anthropic.*

### Anthropic's shares closed 17% above the IPO price after a volatile first day
<span class="meta">Products & companies</span>

Anthropic's first trade on Nasdaq, shortly before noon on 19 November, was at $111.50, 34% above the $83 IPO price ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/live/anthropic-ipo-live-anthropic-stock-begins-trading-in-record-81b-raise-with-15t-valuation-192410598.html); [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/stock-market-today-live-november-19-2026)). The shares peaked near $115, then fell about 13% in the first half hour, which Yahoo calls the worst start for a big US IPO since 2008. They closed at $97.17, up 17% on the day ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/19/stock-market-today-nov-19-dow-plunges-1-100-points-as-the-s-and-p-500-suffers-its-6th-straight-day-of-losses/)), and slipped to about $92.60 in after-hours trading. Valuation figures differ by share count: Yahoo puts the close at about $1.27 trillion on 13.1 billion shares outstanding, while CNBC, cited by StockTitan, put the opening price at roughly $2 trillion fully diluted.

```
  ANT on day one (IPO price $83)
  $115 ┤   ● peak ~$115
  $111 ┤  ● first trade $111.50 (+34%)
  $97  ┤        ╲___________● close $97.17 (+17%)
  $93  ┤                         ● after hours ~$92.60
  $83  ┼──────────────────────────────── IPO price
        noon                 4pm      8pm
```

**Why it matters:** buyers who got shares at $83 made a quick profit, but the fall from the open shows the public was less eager than the first minutes suggested. The early wobble, plus the many staggered lock-up expiries Yahoo notes in the filing, means the price could stay jumpy for months as insiders become free to sell.

### Stocks fell 2% as the sell-off spread beyond tech
<span class="meta">Enabling tech</span>

The S&P 500 fell about 2% on 19 November for a sixth straight loss, the Dow dropped about 1,100 points (2.2%) and the Nasdaq fell about 2.3% ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/11/19/stock-market-today-nov-19-dow-plunges-1-100-points-as-the-s-and-p-500-suffers-its-6th-straight-day-of-losses/); [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/stock-market-today-live-november-19-2026)). Nvidia fell 3.3% and Alphabet, which owns a large Anthropic stake, fell about 3%. StockTitan, citing CNBC, says investors took profits after Anthropic opened and that the IPO failed to revive enthusiasm for AI stocks; the Fool points to worries about inflation, higher interest rates and valuations, with industrials hit hardest.

**Why it matters:** the record IPO did not lift the rest of the AI trade. Money that went into Anthropic may partly have come out of the chip and cloud companies that sell to it, and the expected December rate rise keeps pressure on the most capital-hungry parts of the build-out.

### Jensen Huang will face three House committees on 1 December and has agreed to a public Senate hearing
<span class="meta">Geopolitics</span>

Nvidia's former chief executive will give closed-door, transcribed interviews on 1 December to the House China select committee and the Foreign Affairs and Intelligence committees, with Nvidia's general counsel following on 2 December ([SCMP via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/11/19/jensen-huang-agrees-to-face-congress-over-nvidia-scandal); [Just The News](https://justthenews.com/government/security/nvidia-spy-scandal-spurs-push-tighter-china-chip-export-controls-huang-set)). Separately, Huang and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick have agreed to appear before the Senate Banking Committee in public. The committee asked for 3 December for Huang and 10 December for Lutnick, but final dates are still being discussed ([24/7 Wall St via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/jensen-huang-face-senators-first-142852627.html); [The Wealth Advisor](https://www.thewealthadvisor.com/article/nvidias-jensen-huang-and-commerce-secretary-lutnick-face-public-senate-hearings-over-china)). Several bipartisan bills to tighten chip exports to China could be attached to the annual defence bill before year end. China and the Singapore company at the centre of the case deny wrongdoing.

```
  Who faces Congress, and how
  1 Dec   Huang       House (3 committees)   closed door   confirmed
  2 Dec   Nvidia GC   House                  closed door   confirmed
  ~3 Dec  Huang       Senate Banking         public        date requested
  ~10 Dec Lutnick     Senate Banking         public        date requested
```

**Why it matters:** the case has moved from one company's scandal to a question about how Commerce licenses chip sales, with the department's own secretary called to explain. Putting export rules into the must-pass defence bill would make them law rather than agency policy, which is harder for any future administration to loosen.

*Correction to yesterday's radar: the House appearance is on 1 December, after Thanksgiving, not before it.*

## On the radar

- **3 and 10 December (requested):** public Senate Banking hearings with Huang and Lutnick.
